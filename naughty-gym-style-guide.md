# The Naughty Gym Show: edit style guide

Built from episodes #88 and #89 (Oct 2026): the RSS audio, YouTube thumbnails and cover art.

## Format
- Hosts April and Scott. Weekly, about 53 to 63 minutes. Some episodes have a guest. Mono 44.1 kHz, about -19 LUFS integrated.
- Rebranding: "The Naughty Gym Show, becoming AYA Society" (official name from January).

## Intro (about 51 s)
1. Cold-open narration over a music bed, 0:00 to about 0:48: "We are April and Scott, creators of NaughtyGym.com, and this is The Naughty Gym Show… hop on the treadmill, start the show, and let us teach you how to never stop playing."
2. A music sting of about 2 s.
3. April: "Welcome back to The Naughty Gym Show (soon to be AYA Society). I'm your host April and this is my co-host Scott."
4. Housekeeping: like, subscribe and leave a 5-star review; the AYA Society pitch (promo code AYA, first month free, $19.95/mo after); upcoming events/retreats.
5. Topic setup, then the guest intro if there is one.

## Outro
- Wrap-up: "So those are our thoughts for today", then a repeat of like/subscribe/review, the Wild Love Theory book on Amazon, and naughtygym.com / AYASociety.com.
- Guest episodes: "How can people find you?" plug, then thank the guest.
- Sign-off: "Thanks for listening… remember to **never stop playing**." Then about 20 s of outro music.

## Visual look
- Set: a deep olive-green velvet curtain, a cream bouclé sofa, black podcast mics, and a black tray with a gold/green glass.
- Thumbnail layout: a split two-shot with a black vertical center band; huge white condensed italic all-caps title; "EPISODE #NN" in a small olive box; "WITH APRIL & SCOTT" in white sans-serif bold caps on an olive box; the gold serif logo stacked at the bottom.
- Palette (sampled):
  - Olive curtain `#45503D`
  - Dark olive label `#3A3D2D`
  - Logo gold `#BBA26F`
  - Black `#000000`
  - Cream `#D2C5C2`
  - White `#FFFFFF`
- Type: the logo is a high-contrast serif in gold, with "BECOMING" in small italic serif caps. Titles use a condensed bold italic sans (Bebas/Anton style).
- Vibe: warm, moody, upscale lounge. Lots of laughter; frank and playful, not clinical.

## Edit rules I'll apply
- Cut filler words (um, uh, like-as-filler), false starts, dead air over about 0.6 s, and crosstalk restarts. Keep natural breaths and laughs, because the banter is part of the brand.
- Cut boring sections: logistics tangents, repeated points, scheduling or tech talk, and long rambles that lose the thread. Keep the jokes and teasing between April and Scott.
- Keep the standard intro and outro beats above. Put the event plugs at the front, as on recent episodes.
- Master to about -19 LUFS, matching the feed.
